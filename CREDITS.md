# Image credits and provenance

Every photograph on this site was downloaded and committed to `/img/`. Nothing is
hotlinked to a third-party CDN, so nothing here can break when a photographer
deletes a photo or a platform re-paths its URLs.

**Licensing.** All images are Unsplash License or Pexels License. Both permit
commercial use on a business website, and **neither requires attribution**. This
file exists anyway — it is the evidence of provenance if anyone ever asks, and it
is what lets a specific image be re-sourced or replaced later.

**Downloaded:** 2 August 2026. Each file was fetched at the exact display
dimensions used on the page, re-encoded (JPEG q80 progressive, WebP q78), and
stripped of EXIF metadata.

## Honesty rule — read before changing any caption

**No photograph here shows Danny Dalton's roof, his trays, or his operation.**
He does not have a roof yet; finding one is what the site is for.

Every image is therefore captioned `REFERENCE` on the page, and no caption uses a
possessive. This is not a stylistic choice. A building manager who reverse-image-
searches a photo captioned "our rooftop" and finds it on Pexels discards the
entire page, including the parts that are true — and the parts that are true are
the argument.

When Danny has real photographs, replace the file in `/img/` and change the plate
token from `REFERENCE` to `W 85TH ST`. Both changes must happen together.

Two images are **not New York** and must never be captioned as such:

- `fig-06-rooftop-grower` — shot in Amritsar, India. Captioned by location on the page.
- (Pexels 11601342, an urban rooftop farm, was rejected during sourcing for the same reason.)
