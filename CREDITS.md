# Image credits and provenance

Every photograph on this site was downloaded and committed to `/img/`. Nothing is
hotlinked to a third-party CDN, so nothing here can break when a photographer
deletes a photo or a platform re-paths its URLs.

**Licensing.** The `fig-*` images are Unsplash License or Pexels License. Both
permit commercial use on a business website, and **neither requires
attribution**. This file exists anyway — it is the evidence of provenance if
anyone ever asks, and it is what lets a specific image be re-sourced or replaced
later. The `own-*` images were supplied by Danny and are covered separately in
the next section; five of them have no established provenance.

**Downloaded:** 2 August 2026. Each file was fetched at the exact display
dimensions used on the page, re-encoded (JPEG q80 progressive, WebP q78), and
stripped of EXIF metadata.

## Honesty rule — read before changing any caption

**No photograph here shows Danny Dalton's roof, his trays, or his operation**,
apart from the portrait of Danny himself. He does not have a roof yet;
finding one is what the site is for.

Every other image is therefore captioned `REFERENCE` on the page, and no caption
uses a possessive. This is not a stylistic choice. A building manager who
reverse-image-searches a photo captioned "our rooftop" and finds it on Pexels
discards the entire page, including the parts that are true — and the parts that
are true are the argument.

When Danny has real photographs of the growing operation, replace the file in
`/img/` and change the plate token from `REFERENCE` to `W 85TH ST`. Both changes
must happen together. The portrait already uses a `My own photograph` plate,
which is the pattern to follow.
